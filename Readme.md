
# Water Quality Classification | 3rd Place - IBD EQR Project

**Team:** Nuria Arroyo, Heriberto Espino, José Ángel Palomares, Gil Andrei Sotomayor.

## Achievement
Awarded **3rd Place** for developing a predictive model to classify water bodies in France based on biological and chemical indicators.

## The Challenge
The goal was to accurately categorize water samples using complex environmental datasets. We analyzed:
* **Biological Data:** Taxon presence and diversity.
* **Chemical Data:** Mineral concentrations and pollutants.
* **Geospatial Data:** Coordinates and size of the water bodies.

## Methodology
* **Feature Engineering:** Handling high-dimensional data (taxons) and spatial features.
* **Clustering & Classification:** Implementing algorithms to group water samples by quality and health indicators.
* **Geospatial Analysis:** Integrating location data to identify regional patterns in water degradation.


* **Concepts:** Supervised Learning, Environmental Data Science.
